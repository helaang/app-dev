#  When Life Gives You Tangerine

**When Life Gives You Tangerine** is a story about turning hard times into sweet memories.  
It’s full of life lessons, friendship, and love.

---

## What Makes It Special for me

- **Fun** and *emotional* storytelling
-  Relatable characters and strong friendships turns to love
-  Bright colors and amazing transition of story
-  Deep quotes that make you think the deep definition of it

> *“Sometimes, the sour things in life lead to the sweetest changes.”*

---
## Special Characters That Matters to me

- **Yang Gwan-sik (Park Bo Gum)** the main male character and Ae-sun's love interest, boyfriend and later husband, and he shows how pain can be hidden behind silence.
- **Oh Ae-sun(IU)** the main female character and Gwan-sik's love interest, girlfriend and later wife, and as Yang Geum-myeong, the eldest child and only daughter of Ae-sun and Gwan-sik and she reminds us that kindness can heal.


> _"Sometimes, you meet people who help you peel away your sadness."_
